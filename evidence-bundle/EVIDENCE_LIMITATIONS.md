# Evidence Limitations

This curated bundle is intentionally small. It is meant to support supervisor
inspection of the KCD1 CPU-affinity non-validation result without requiring the
full raw archive.

Limitations:

- One machine and one real-game route.
- Preliminary KCD1 A/B data was paired but not counterbalanced.
- No general performance claim is made for KCD1, Proton, Linux gaming, or CPU
  affinity.
- The supported claim is methodological: the workflow could inspect a plausible
  tuning hypothesis and decline it when evidence did not support the candidate.
- Sanitized placeholders such as `<home>`, `<user>`, and `<repo>` are
  intentional in presentation artifacts.
