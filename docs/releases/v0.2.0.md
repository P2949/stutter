# Stutter v0.2.0

The first release with a Supported autonomous controller, bounded to exactly the two contracts below. It is not a general system-wide optimizer.

## Supported

Observation (unchanged from v0.1.0): `doctor`, `record`, `bench`, `report`, `audit`, `completions_and_man`.

Bounded autonomous tuning (new): `cpu_affinity_experiment`, `cgroup_placement_experiment` and the controller that applies them, `bounded_autotune_controller`. The controller authorises exactly the entries of the autonomous support registry, enforced at runtime:

- CPU-affinity profile: a stable, explicitly identified game under scheduler pressure, judged by the target's own runnable latency (`GameRunnableLatency`), local process-tree scope.
- Cgroup placement: the same workload and objective, into the administrator-configured game cgroup.

Anything absent from that registry is suggest-only, benchmark-only, manual or rejected. Each applied experiment is prepared before the first write (journal-before-write two-phase transaction), physically verified, rolled back unless it is a measured improvement, audited, and recovered after a crash.

## Experimental (not Supported)

- The daemon/service always-on integration (`always_on_autotune`). The packaged service runs `apply-low-risk`; the controller's evidence is for `stutter autotune --mode apply-medium-risk`.
- uclamp, nice and I/O priority (evaluated, DeferredEvidence), CPU EPP and governor, GPU power profile and forced performance level, VM swappiness and dirty ratios, known-device and storage/unknown IRQ affinity (evaluated, DeferredEvidence or manual/HighRisk), and remote agent mutation. High-risk apply is not implemented.
- Diagnosis, the advisor, StutterScore and `tune_evaluation`, as in v0.1.0.

## Evidence

- The bounded controller graduated on a coverage-qualified real-system soak at one exact commit: at least 8 h, 113 of 113 rounds clean, 7 complete clean 16-round cycles, every dangerous event kind (controller crash and stop, worker kill, suspend/resume, workload kill, focus swap, long rounds) clean at least 6 times, both operations repeatedly kept and reverted, and all zero-violation checks passing. It was backed by a 10,000-seed deterministic fault sweep and a real-controller SIGINT stress in `ci-live`.
- Two earlier soaks found real defects (a prepared-apply race and a lost SIGINT); they were fixed and their runs discarded.
- The soak used disposable game-like workloads, not real games.
- Observed kernels and degraded behaviour are as documented in `docs/KERNEL_COMPATIBILITY.md`.

## Verification

The release package was verified with `xtask release-verify --require-acceptance` against fresh evidence bundles taken at the exact release commit (observation and the production-controller smoke), and the installed package passed `xtask release-install-smoke`. Verify downloads with `sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS` and read `SUPPORT.md` first.
