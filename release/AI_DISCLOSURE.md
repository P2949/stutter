# AI Use Disclosure

This prototype was developed with AI coding-assistant support, including Claude,
ChatGPT/OpenAI tooling, and Codex-style review workflows where applicable.

These tools were used as review and pair-programming support: to critique code,
suggest implementation alternatives, identify possible bugs, draft candidate
patches for review, and help refine documentation.

AI assistance was not treated as authoritative. All accepted design decisions,
code changes, experimental setup, benchmark execution, validation runs,
interpretation of results, and final project conclusions remain the
responsibility of the author. AI-generated or AI-suggested material was reviewed,
adapted, tested, and either accepted or rejected by the author before inclusion.

The proposed assessed FYP contribution is the author's methodology,
implementation, validation, and reporting work. This disclosure is included to
make the nature of AI assistance explicit for supervisor review and to align with
academic-integrity expectations.
